Stupid Tears

(Lagrimas Estupidas. Crying and annoyed about it.)

July 2026  ·  L8 Blo0om

Have you ever cried over someone and been mad at your own face for doing it? That's the song.

Lagrimas estupidas. Stupid tears. Not because sadness is stupid — because these particular tears were being wasted on someone who absolutely did not deserve the moisture. My eyes and I were not in agreement that night, and I wrote the whole argument down.

"My mascara died for someone who wouldn't even text back. Ridiculous."

It's the crying-but-laughing-about-it song. The one you play when you're 70% healed and can finally roll your eyes at yourself. Grief and comedy live closer together than anyone admits.
