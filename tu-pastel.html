Your Cake

(Tu Pastel. Sweet, and you know it.)

July 2026  ·  L8 Blo0om

You can't have your cake and eat it too. Unless the cake leaves first.

Tu pastel. Your cake. This one's flirty on the surface and a warning underneath. It's about being the sweet thing someone took for granted — the dessert they kept saving for later, assuming it would still be on the table whenever they got around to it.

"You left the cake out. The cake found a better party."

It's playful because I was in a playful mood when the pettiness struck. Some revenge songs scream. This one just smiles and licks the frosting off its own finger.
