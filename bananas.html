Bananas

(Everybody loves Saturday night.)

July 2026  ·  L8 Blo0om

This is the sound of a Saturday night that gets away from you in the best possible way.

Paper-bag players, a sip from the flask, a sing-along that starts in one language and ends in another — tout le monde aime samedi soir. Somewhere in the middle of all that noise, the song hits its real note: free. Said as many times as it takes to believe it.

Yes, that chorus is exactly as fun to sing as it looks.
