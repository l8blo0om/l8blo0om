Vault Tips
FDO Framework: Genre → Tempo/Key → Vocal chain → Reverb → Spatial/Panning → EQ warmth → Dynamics → Drums → Instruments → Mood.
Heritage Framework: Production level → Era/Location → Studio/Label → Producer → Gear → Drums/Bass → Vocals → Always-on tags → Compression → FX.
Low-Pass + Tape Saturation: Rolls off harsh highs and adds warmth without muddying the low end.
Heritage secret: Real studio names, producer names, and gear references train Suno's style model toward authentic vintage sounds.
Both frameworks always include: side chain, 3D, Binaural — these are non-negotiable for spatial depth.
