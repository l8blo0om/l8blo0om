Vault Tips
Style field: Paste the generated prompt here. Keep it under 1000 characters.
Lyrics field: Use structure tags like [Intro], [Verse], [Chorus], [Bridge], [Outro] to control song architecture.
Earworm tip: Repeat the hook phrase in both Verse and Chorus sections for max memorability.
Low-Pass + Tape Saturation: These two together roll off harsh highs and add warmth without muddying the low end.
Panning Automation + Mid-Side: Use together for a wide, breathing stereo image that translates well on streaming.
Negative Space: Silence is a production tool. Sparse beats with deliberate gaps hit harder than busy ones.
