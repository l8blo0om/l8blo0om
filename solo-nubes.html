Only Clouds

(Solo Nubes. Sometimes nothing is the whole point.)

July 2026  ·  L8 Blo0om

Some songs are about a person. This one is about the absence of everything.

Solo nubes. Only clouds. No drama, no ex texting at 2am, no plot. Just sky. I wrote it on one of those days where nothing was wrong for once and my first reaction was suspicion — like, okay, what's coming?

"Peace felt so unfamiliar I almost asked it for ID."

Then I let it be what it was. A day of only clouds. The song floats on purpose — it's the sound of finally exhaling. If you need four minutes of that, this one's yours.
